Sudo Stretch

Break reminders for developers. A Mac menu bar app.

Every 30 or 60 minutes Sudo Stretch reminds you to take a short break, with one simple exercise for your hands, eyes or body. Answer the reminder with Done, Snooze or Skip, or do the exercise in a small window with a short looping demo.

  • Lives in the menu bar, next to the clock, with the minutes left until the next break.
  • Stepped away or the Mac slept? That counts as a break, and the countdown starts over.
  • On a call? The reminder waits until it ends.
  • Pause for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you resume.
  • Optional exercises follow NHS advice for people who sit a lot.

Free. No account, no network, no data collected: everything stays on your Mac.
