Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please mention your macOS version and what you expected to happen.

I can't find the app

Sudo Stretch has no Dock icon: it lives in the menu bar, next to the clock, as a cup with the minutes left until the next break. On a Mac with a notch the icon can hide behind it — hold ⌘ and drag it closer to the clock.

Reminders disappear too fast

macOS shows new apps' notifications as temporary banners. To keep a reminder on screen with its buttons until you answer, open System Settings › Notifications › Sudo Stretch and choose the Persistent style (on macOS 14 and 15: Alerts). Sudo Stretch → Settings… has a button that opens that page.

I get no reminders

  • Check System Settings › Notifications › Sudo Stretch: notifications must be allowed.
  • A Focus mode may hide them; allow Sudo Stretch in that Focus if you want reminders during it.
  • The menu shows whether reminders are paused, and a Resume item if they are.
  • While another app uses the microphone (a call), the reminder waits up to 30 minutes.

Open at login

Turn on Open at login in Sudo Stretch → Settings…. If macOS asks for approval, allow it in System Settings › General › Login Items.

Does it need an administrator password?

No — despite the name. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and only ever asks you to stretch.
