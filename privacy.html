Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Sudo Stretch does not collect any data. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What the app stores, and where

Your settings (break interval, exercise categories, tone of messages, pause) and a small journal of your breaks (when a reminder was shown and whether you did, snoozed or skipped the exercise) are kept only on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. They are used only to schedule reminders, rotate exercises and choose motivational lines. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • No network connections: the app is built without network access.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your camera, contacts, location or files.

Microphone

To hold a reminder during a call, the app asks macOS only whether some app is using the microphone. It never records or receives any audio and has no microphone permission.

Links

The exercise window and the About panel link to the public sources of facts and exercises (studies, NHS pages). They open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
